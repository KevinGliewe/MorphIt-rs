    Surface error is the unsigned distance from mesh-surface sample points to the nearest sphere surface (8,000 samples per part), the paper's boundary-fidelity metric. Coverage is the fraction of mesh-interior Monte Carlo samples inside the sphere union, the paper's spatial-occupancy (inside) ratio. In robot mode the overall row is area-weighted for distances and volume-weighted for coverage. The heatmap colors the original geometry by surface error on a light-to-dark single-hue scale.
